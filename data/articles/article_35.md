# Article 35. Right relating to h ealth: (1) Every citizen shall have the right to free basic 

health services from the State, and n o o ne shall be deprived of emergency 
health services. 
(2) Every person shall have the right to get information about his or 
her medical treatment.

(4) Every citi zen shall have the right of access to clean drinking 
water and sanitation.
